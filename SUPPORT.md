# Elite Footballer Support

Need help with Elite Footballer? Here's how to get it.

## Contact us

Open an issue on GitHub: https://github.com/thomastelintelo/soccer-training/issues

Please include:
- What you were doing and what happened
- Your iPhone model and iOS version
- Screenshots, if they help

## Common questions

**How do I join a team?**
Ask your coach for the team's join code, then enter it on the Team tab.

**How do I leave a team?**
Open your team in the Team tab and choose Leave Team. You'll be removed from the team's member list.

**My progress is missing.**
Training progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

**How do I cancel Elite Footballer Plus?**
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Elite Footballer.

**Camera or microphone isn't working for screen recording.**
Enable access in iOS Settings > Elite Footballer.
