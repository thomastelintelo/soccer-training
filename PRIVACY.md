# Elite Footballer Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

Elite Footballer ("the app") is a soccer training app for players and coaches. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

## Information we do not collect

We do not run our own servers, require an account, or ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

## Information stored on your device

Your training progress, completed drills, test results, achievements, profile and player card details are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this information. Deleting the app removes it.

## Team features (coaches and players)

If you create or join a team, the app stores team information in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) so teammates can see it. This includes the team name, join code, member display names and profile details you enter, and drill assignments and schedules. Anyone with a team's join code can join that team and view this shared information, so please only share join codes with people you trust and avoid entering sensitive personal details. Leaving a team removes you from that team's member list. This data is handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Camera and microphone

The app can use your camera and microphone only when you choose to record a screen-recording video. Recordings are saved by you on your own device. We do not upload or receive them.

## Purchases

Elite Footballer Plus is an auto-renewing annual subscription processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple manages billing, renewals, and cancellation in your Apple Account settings.

## Children's privacy

The app is used by young players. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians should supervise team features and decide what profile details a child enters.

## Your choices

You can delete the app to remove local data, leave a team at any time, and revoke camera and microphone access in iOS Settings. To ask about team data stored in iCloud, contact us at the address below.
